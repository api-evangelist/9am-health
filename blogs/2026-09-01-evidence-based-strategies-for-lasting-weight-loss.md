To best personalize your weight maintenance plan, it can be helpful to first identify your own keys to success. Looking back on your weight-loss journey, ask yourself: What lifestyle changes have you made that have contributed to your weight loss to date? It can be helpful to look at your past records, logs, notes, and goals to review and assess.
