If you've spent years struggling with your weight, chances are you've also spent years criticizing yourself. Many people have an inner voice that says things like, "I'll never look good," "I should do better," or "I look horrible." Over time, these thoughts can become automatic, making it difficult to appreciate yourself—even when you're making progress.
